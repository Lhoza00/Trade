Install and offline

Android (Chrome): menu, then "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
iPhone (Safari): Share button, then "Add to Home Screen". Safari can clear a website's saved data after a week of not opening it, and installing it to the Home Screen avoids that.

Erase

Removes every entry, picture, check-in and library item from this phone. Download a backup first if you might want them again.
